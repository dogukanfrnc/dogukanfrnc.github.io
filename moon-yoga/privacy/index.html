Privacy Policy

How Moon Yoga handles your information.

Effective 5 October 2026. Applies to Moon Yoga on iOS.

The short version

Moon Yoga keeps everything you write and track on your own device. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server that collects your practice, your journal or your moods. We cannot read your entries, because they never reach us. The free version shows ads from Google, which collects some device information to show them — the Advertising section below explains what, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Nothing you write or track is sent to us. Moon Yoga does not ask for your name in order to identify you, does not request your email address and does not use analytics software. The free version includes Google’s advertising software, described under Advertising below; it can track you across other companies’ apps and websites only if you allow it.

The name you choose during onboarding is only a greeting. It is stored on your device and used nowhere else.

Where your information lives

Your profile, preferences, journal entries, moods, streaks, milestones and moonstones are saved in the app’s private storage on your device, using the operating system’s standard app preferences.

If you have iCloud Backup or an encrypted device backup switched on, that storage may be included in your own backup, under your Apple Account and Apple’s terms. We have no access to those backups.

Deleting Moon Yoga removes this data from your device. You can also erase it from inside the app: Settings, then Delete all my data.

Reminders and notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally by your device at the time you choose. No push server is involved and no notification content leaves your phone. You can turn reminders off at any time in iOS Settings or by changing your reminder preference in the app.

Subscriptions and payments

Moon Yoga Luna is sold and billed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We never see or store your card number, billing address, Apple Account details or any other payment information.

To confirm whether your subscription is active, the app may use a subscription-management service that receives only your App Store receipt and a randomly generated, app-specific identifier. That identifier is not linked to your name, your email or your journal.

Advertising

The free version of Moon Yoga shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob, delivered by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. They appear only at natural pauses, such as before or after a practice, and never during one. Moon Yoga Luna members see no ads: for them the advertising software is never started.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers (the Identifier for Advertisers, or IDFA, only if you allow tracking), your approximate location estimated from your IP address, how you interact with ads (for example ads shown, taps and video views), and diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance information. Google uses this to serve ads, to measure how they perform and to prevent fraud and abuse.

Before Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt appears, a short note in the app explains why it is asked; the prompt itself asks you once. If you choose not to allow tracking, Google cannot use your advertising identifier and the ads you see are less personalised. You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choices where the law requires it. Whenever this applies, you can review them in Settings, under Privacy choices.

We do not receive this information ourselves; AdMob only shows us overall reports, such as how many ads were seen. How Google uses information from apps that use its services is explained at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not hold any. We do not build advertising profiles ourselves, and we do not ask for permission to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location or health data. The free version’s ads are the one place where a third party, Google, collects information for advertising; the Advertising section explains it.

Children

Moon Yoga is intended for a general audience of teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. As no information reaches us, there is nothing for a parent to request or have deleted on our side; anything stored can be erased on the device itself.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any journal entry at any time. You can reset your profile and start the welcome flow again, or erase everything the app has stored, from Settings. Because the data is yours and local, these actions are immediate and permanent — we cannot undo them for you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear here in the app and at the public policy address, with a new effective date. Continuing to use Moon Yoga after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy can go to halehdf1@gmail.com. You can copy that address from Settings, under Support.
